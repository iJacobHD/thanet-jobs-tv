Thanet Jobs TV – updating the board

Takes about 2 minutes. Do it at least twice a week (e.g. Monday and Thursday).

One-time setup

Drag this yellow button onto your browser's bookmarks bar. If you can't see the bar, press Ctrl+Shift+B.

Collect Thanet jobs

Each time

  1. Open Work Hub.
  2. Click the Collect Thanet jobs bookmark. A blue box appears in the corner. Wait about a minute until it says Done. A file called thanet-jobs-DATE.json is saved to your Downloads.
  3. Open the upload page, drag the file in, then press Commit changes.

    Open the upload page
  4. About 5 minutes later, the TV board and the USB slides are up to date.
  5. USB TV only: put the stick in a PC, double-click Update USB stick.bat, then put the stick back in the TV.
The board only shows jobs that are in Thanet, still open, entry level and not self-employed. Jobs past their closing date drop off by themselves. The bookmark only reads public job adverts; it doesn't change anything on Work Hub. To see why a job was left out, check the report.
